youtube

YouTube is an American online video-sharing platform owned by Google. YouTube was founded on February 14, 2005, by Chad Hurley, Jawed Karim, and Steve Chen, all of whom were former employees at PayPal. [7] Headquartered in San Bruno, California, it is the second-most-visited website in the world, after Google itself. In January 2024, YouTube had reached more than 2. 7 billion monthly active users, who collectively watched more than one billion hours of video every day. [8] As of May 2019, videos were being uploaded to the platform at a rate of more than 500 hours of video per minute,[9][10] and as of mid-2024, there were approximately 14. 8 billion videos in total.
